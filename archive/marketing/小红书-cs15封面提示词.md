### 2.1 正向 Prompt

```
Vertical 3:4 xiaohongshu cover, 1080x1440 portrait.

SUBJECT / MAIN VISUAL (Heavy zone, 60%+ of frame):
First-person shooter POV inside a CS-style tactical map corridor.
Right side of frame: player's own hands gripping a matte-black AK-47 rifle,
barrel pointing toward the center-left, subtle muzzle glow.
Center: bright green crosshair reticle aimed at a distant enemy silhouette
standing at the end of the corridor between wooden crate cover blocks.
Environment: checkerboard concrete floor, wooden crates (full-height and half-height),
dusty volumetric light shafts, atmospheric haze fading into the background,
bluish-dark tactical lighting with warm amber rim light.
Deep depth of field applied to background only; gun, crosshair and enemy
remain tack sharp — atmosphere must never erase the subject.

TITLE TEXT (Medium zone, large, bold):
Bold condensed Chinese headline "离谱！前端大佬手搓 CS" rendered
horizontally across the upper-center area, occupying >60% of frame width,
huge display size with amber-orange (#D97035) heavy outline + white fill,
strong drop shadow for legibility over the scene.
Directly below it, a smaller white subtitle line:
"双击就能玩 · 还带爆头判定" (40-60pt).

CORNER TAG BADGES (Light zone, small capsule chips):
Top-left chip: "零下载" — Top-right chip: "爆头×4" —
Bottom-left chip: "手机可玩" — Bottom-right chip: "8 代号 AI".
Each chip = dark translucent capsule (#0D0D12 at 70% opacity) with
amber-orange border and white text, angled slightly.

BRAND ELEMENT:
At bottom-center, brand text "CS 1.5" appears exactly once, styled as
green crosshair + gold numeral, small and unobtrusive.

COLOR PALETTE (strict hex fidelity):
Background base #0D0D12 (dominant ~60%), primary accent amber-orange
#D97035 (~25%), secondary teal #3D8996 for crosshair/glow (~10%),
parchment white #E9E5D6 for text (~5%).
Warm-to-cool cinematic tonal gradient flowing from a luminous lighter
upper zone through the text band into the rich dark heavy lower scene —
seamless tonal transition, NO horizontal color split, NO color blocks.

COMPOSITION PHILOSOPHY:
Fully immersive composition: scene fills the entire frame edge-to-edge,
title text floats as an overlay layer with stroke/shadow support,
no separate text zone, no rectangular background block behind text,
no geometric division of the canvas.

TEXT RENDERING DISCIPLINE:
Each text element appears exactly once in the composition,
no duplicated copy, no repeated phrases,
Chinese title only, no English translation overlay.

STYLE LOCK:
Stylized 3D game-art cinematic render (C4D + Octane feel), AAA FPS game
key-art quality, high-energy composition, dramatic contrast lighting,
slight film grain.
```

## 五、使用建议

1. **首选 gpt-image-2（openai）或 qwen**：直接渲染方案二，中文 11 字标题在稳定渲染范围内
2. 生成后自检：眯眼看图应是一张整体图（非上下两块）；标题字缩略图下仍可读；主体未被雾效吞掉
3. 若渲染英文替换文字：可换英文标题 "CS in Your Browser?"（≤6 词可直嵌）
4. 发布前确认：标题只出现一次、角标未重叠、底部 10% 无关键信息（避免被小红书 UI bar 遮挡）
